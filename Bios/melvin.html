    Name: Melvin O'Hare

    ID: He/him

    Species: Bunny

    Genre(s): Air guitar

A death metal fanatic who runs The Candled society's "Super Awesome Metal Club" alongside his inseparable, also death metal obsessed fox friend Boris, though Melvin prefers his death metal more chaotic, loud, and gross, always looking forward to new, modern releases. Melvin could talk and talk about his new death metal findings to one all day, leading to the creation of his and Boris' club, even if Billy and Candy are its only other members. Melvin, and Boris resultingly, is the member of The Candled most immersed in city life and culture, frequenting visits out of the forest and famously being the only candled animal to own a computer.
