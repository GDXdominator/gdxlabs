    Name: Melvin and Boris

    ID: They/them

    Species: Bunny and red fox

    Genre(s): Air guitar

Two death metal and thrash metal obsessed nerds who run the Candled society's “Super Awesome Metal Club” and are the first of The Candled society to have access to the internet, even if primitively so. Boris and Melvin met on one fateful day in the elementary school yard, both having just discovered what an AC/DC and Metallica was. From there, their friendship grew as did the extremity of their taste in metal. An even more fateful day would be their discovery of a majestic link that downloaded mp3s of Cannibal Corpse's Tomb of the Mutilated, a sound that their rather gargantuan ears had never heard before, but were graced by and would set them on left-hand path to death metal. The two are well acquainted with Candy and Billy, both being the only other members of the Super Awesome Metal Club and possessing tastes in extreme music to match, even while misaligned with theirs. Despite having no knowledge of how to play any instruments, that hasn't stopped them from impressively mastering how to shred on the air guitar.
