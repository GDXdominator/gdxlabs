    Genre(s): Witchcrap + fantasy, dungeon synth, ambient

An adventurous, mischievous witch in training exploring the bizarre lands of his dimension, filled with dreams held dear since childhood of being a musician and a magical wizard with a love for all things mystical. Lives atop a secluded, tall tower on Purple Hill like any good witch would under the apprenticeship of grand old wizard Hinkleheimer, though he is enamored by both the wonderful and undiscovered world(s) surrounding him, and thus became a wanderer at heart, writing tunes about his quests and adventures. May or may not be just GDX but purple with a witch costume and a bad case of RBF. Many theories are thrown around by the lab about how those two both exist and who they are.
