    Name: GDX

    Species: Stickman

    Genre(s): Hardcore EDM + digital hardcore, speedcore, beep boop sounds, broken printer noises

An erratic, so-called mad scientist who will throw random chemicals into vials to see if they do something cool, much like his musical process. A childhood of dubstep, a teenagehood of digital hardcore and metal, maybe growing up next to a construction zone, and perhaps too much time on the internet all came together to form this doofus bent on pushing the range of his EDM. Has permanently damaged his taste in music and now loves his music loud, fast, and noisy. Formed GDX Labs to make music with other freaks.
