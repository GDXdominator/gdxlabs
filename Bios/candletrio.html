    Name: The Candimals

    ID: They/them

    Species: Numerous

    Genre(s): Sounds of the forest

(Also known as the Candle Trio) A forest-dwelling trio consisting of Candy, Billy, and Benny (Candle Cat, Candle Goat, and Candle Deer), three lonesome wanderers who live in the Forest of Doom (and Darkness), a forest of many creatures such as them. The three are part of a larger ragtag group of candled animals, who are all rather juvenile and are the first of The Candled, a society who had long chosen to stay far from modern living, to explore the modern world, city life, and—most importantly—rock music. The three have adopted The Candled's old traditional robes, worn in worship of the now deceased Idol, with Candy being the first, Benny tagging along, and Billy following along for aesthetics.
