    Name: lucy

    Real name: Lucy Mya Meowserton

    ID: She/her

    Species: Black cat stickman thing

    Genre(s): Jungle + DnB, breakbeat

A proudly 7-foot tall black cat humanoid with a delightful, almost never-ending smile and rectangular eyes with either a million or zero thoughts behind them. Lucy in her childhood was enchanted by the tunes on her Nintendo 64 games and her dad's collection of jungle tapes. She would begin to fiddle with a crusty Yamaha keyboard, sampler, and zippers that mimicked a DJ's glorious record scratching, eventually leading her to the drum kit, of which she became a devoted follower of. Lucy randomly showed up in the lab one day, where she and GDX quickly got along through their shared love of drums and DJing and comparable levels of moronicism, becoming the first official member of GDX Labs aside from GDX and Hill Witch. Playful, mischievous, clueless in nature, a talented drummer, will ask you how the weather is down there, and frequently hogs the drum machine and drum kit. Is quite fond of jungle and drum breaks.
