    Name: Candle Cat

    Real name: Candy

    ID: They/them

    Species: Candled American Shorthair Humanoid

    Genre(s): Dark ambient + ambient, drone, drone metal

A shy and reserved cat living in the Forest of Doom (and Darkness) alongside the rest of the Candimals. They are, self admittedly, an anxious wreck. Candy, left to their own devices as a lonely adolescent, became infatuated with the history of The Candled, forest wandering, and the creatures of the forest, inheriting their father's old robe from a time when The Candled still worshipped The Idol and keeping a log of the creatures of the forest. One day, Candy had a spiritual experience at a Sunn O))) concert one day and quickly became infatuated with drone metal and ambient music. Despite being the lab's quietest member, Candle Cat produces the lab's loudest music. They like to spend their time in the lab producing sounds of the forest and soaking in amp feedback. Big advocate for hearing protection. Loves to be pet but is too anxious to ask.
